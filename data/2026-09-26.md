### [1] [Direction-Scale Decomposition in Action Representation: Rethinking What to Tokenize for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28865)
*Yufei Duan,Hang Yin,Alberta Longhini,Chao Tang,Danica Kragic*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出 Direction-Scale Decomposition (DSD) 动作表示，将平移和旋转增量分解为方向与尺度分量后再进行离散 token 化，以提升离散 token VLA 学习的性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 离散 token 的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）学习中，动作表示至关重要但仍研究不足。传统"位姿增量"表示对执行速度和数据集特定的归一化敏感，可能掩盖跨演示和跨数据集共享的几何结构。

Method: 提出 DSD 动作表示：在 token 化之前，将平移与旋转增量分解为方向分量与尺度分量，方向被单独分离，幅度则保留在独立的尺度通道中。采用两种分词器评估：均匀分箱（BIN）和基于 B 样条的 BEAST，并在仿真与真实机器人操作中测试，涵盖单数据集与混合数据集训练。

Result: 在 LIBERO 上，DSD 在两种分词器下均提升平均成功率；在 SimplerEnv 混合数据集训练下，DSD-BIN 的总体成功率超过 BIN 达 10.3 个百分点；真实机器人实验无论是否使用机器人预训练均取得提升。

Conclusion: 结果表明 DSD 是离散 token VLA 模型的有效动作表示，并有望缓解在大规模、多样化数据集混合训练时的性能下降问题。

Abstract: Action representation plays a central role in discrete-token vision-language-action (VLA) learning but remains underexamined. Under conventional pose-increment representations, action tokens are sensitive to execution speed and dataset-specific normalization, potentially obscuring geometric structure shared across demonstrations and datasets. We introduce Direction-Scale Decomposition (DSD), an action representation that decomposes translation and rotation increments into direction and scale components before tokenization. DSD isolates motion direction while retaining magnitudes in separate scale channels. We evaluate DSD with uniform binning (BIN) and BEAST, a B-spline-based tokenizer, in simulation and real-world manipulation under both single-dataset and mixed-dataset training. On LIBERO, DSD improves average success rates with both tokenizers. On SimplerEnv, DSD-BIN outperforms BIN by 10.3 percentage points in overall success rate under mixed-dataset training. Real-robot experiments further show gains both with and without robotics pretraining. These results support DSD as an effective action representation for discrete-token VLA models and suggest its potential to mitigate performance degradation when training on large and diverse dataset mixtures. Our project page with additional resources is available at https://vla-dsd.github.io/

### [2] [DeltaWAM: Delta World Action Models for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28811)
*Han Yan,Zishang Xiang,Haokai Jiang,Zeyu Zhang,Qilin Wang,Weiyu Guo,Yandong Guo,Boxin Shi,Hao Tang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: DeltaWAM 通过预测视觉增量与动作，结合流式增量记忆，提升机器人控制成功率并显著降低训练与推理开销。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有世界-动作模型在训练时预测密集未来帧，反复建模基本不变的内容，并将动作条件动态与无关的外观变化耦合；推理时用重型视频专家处理每个完整观测，成为少步动作生成的瓶颈。

Method: 提出 DeltaWAM，采用密集锚点、稀疏增量与动作三流联合预测视觉增量与动作，并提供三种在表示与计算共享上不同的架构；进一步提出 Streaming Delta Memory（SDM），用紧凑的观测增量更新缓存的锚点上下文，减少重型视频专家处理。

Result: 在 RoboTwin 上，结合 SDM 的 DeltaWAM 将平均成功率从 Fast-WAM 的 81.3% 提升至 85.4%（干净设置），视觉随机化下从 75.8% 提升至 83.9%；三种架构降低训练 FLOPs 17.78–23.77%，SDM 降低单步推理延迟与 FLOPs 分别达 36.57% 与 31.55%；真实世界评测在评估策略中取得最高总体成功率和归一化进度。

Conclusion: DeltaWAM 通过建模视觉增量而非密集帧、并以流式增量记忆降低推理开销，在仿真与真实场景中同时提升成功率与效率。

Abstract: World-action models (WAMs) transfer visual and motion priors from pretrained video generators to robot control by jointly modeling visual dynamics and actions. Existing WAMs, however, predict dense future frames during training, repeatedly modeling largely unchanged content and coupling action-conditioned dynamics to nuisance appearance variations. At inference, processing each complete observation with the heavy video expert bottlenecks few-step action generation. Accordingly, we propose DeltaWAM, which jointly predicts visual deltas and actions using dense-anchor, sparse-delta, and action streams, with three architectures that differ in representation and computation sharing. We further develop Streaming Delta Memory (SDM), which updates cached anchor context with compact observed deltas, reducing heavy video-expert processing. On RoboTwin, DeltaWAM with SDM improves average success over Fast-WAM from 81.3% to 85.4% in the clean setting and from 75.8% to 83.9% under visual randomization. The three architectures reduce training FLOPs by 17.78-23.77%, while SDM reduces one-step inference latency and FLOPs by 36.57% and 31.55%, respectively; real-world evaluations further show the highest overall success rate and normalized progress among the evaluated policies. Code: https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/DeltaWAM. Website: https://aigeeksgroup.github.io/DeltaWAM.

### [3] [Representation World Model: Learning States, Transition and Executable Plans in Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29171)
*Yijun Yuan,Weicheng Zheng,Weibang Wang,Minghui Qin,Chang Sun,Junhao Huang,Kenan Li,Anmin Liu,Yicheng Yao,Hang Zhao*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 提出表示世界模型（RWM），直接在表示空间中学习状态、转移与可执行规划，无需递归推演或动作空间搜索。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有世界模型通常将潜在表示与显式动力学模型一同学习，并通过搜索、优化或基于策略的预测进行规划，规划过程与表示学习分离，效率与结构耦合度不足。

Method: RWM将规划直接嵌入所学表示的几何结构中：通过在由端点表示构建的潜在路径上施加局部逆动力学监督来学习表示几何，使路径保持任务相关的状态与转移信息；推理时直接在当前与目标表示间构建潜在路径，并用逆动力学恢复对应动作。

Result: 在连续控制基准上验证了RWM直接规划的有效性；在机器人操作任务上进一步显示其可扩展到更复杂的具身控制任务。

Conclusion: 在表示空间中直接规划为传统世界模型规划提供了一种有前景的替代方案。

Abstract: We propose the Representation World Model (RWM), which learns states, transitions, and executable plans directly in representation space. Unlike existing world models that typically learn latent representations together with explicit dynamics models and perform planning through search, optimization, or policy-based prediction, RWM directly incorporates planning into the learned representation geometry. RWM learns the representation geometry by applying inverse-dynamics supervision locally along latent paths constructed from endpoint representations, requiring these paths to preserve task-relevant state and transition information. At inference, planning is performed by directly constructing a latent path between the current and goal representations, with inverse dynamics used to recover the corresponding actions, without recursive rollouts or action-space search. Experiments on continuous-control benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of RWM for direct planning, while results on robotic manipulation further show its potential to extend to more complex embodied control tasks. These results suggest that planning directly in representation space provides a promising alternative to conventional world-model planning.

</details>
